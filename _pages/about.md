I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, where I work on LLM agent security, multimodal robustness, and adversarial attacks in physical environments. I completed my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering at PolyU in 2026. My first-author work has appeared at NeurIPS, ICLR, CVPR, and ACM Computing Surveys.

## Research

My research focuses on making AI systems safer and more robust in real-world use:

- **LLM agent security:** runtime safeguards, tool-use authorization, and automated red-teaming.
- **Multimodal robustness:** evaluating prompt injection and unauthorized actions caused by external visual and textual content.
- **Physical-world attacks:** adversarial methods against person detection and 3D face recognition.
- **Generative model security:** style-mimicry prevention and robustness of vision-language models.

I led the dynamic access-control verification work in **GuardAgent** (ICML 2025), which checks whether an agent's tool calls match user intent. I have also contributed to multimodal agent red-teaming projects including **SecureAgent**, and to **UV-Attack** and **SLAttack** on physical and digital robustness. My proposed research agenda, **AgentFence**, studies verifiable runtime authorization for tool-using agents, including policy compilation, authority-expansion detection, least-authority repair, and adaptive evaluation.
