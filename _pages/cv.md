## Selected Research Contributions

- **GuardAgent (ICML 2025):** Led dynamic access-control verification to check agent tool calls against user intent.
- **Multimodal agent red-teaming:** Studied indirect and adaptive prompt injection; developed evaluation scenarios for unauthorized actions induced by external visual and textual content; contributed to SecureAgent.
- **Physical and digital robustness:** Developed dynamic-NeRF UV mapping and structured-light attacks; studied LVLM intermediate projectors and cross-model attack transferability; contributed to SLAttack.
- **AgentFence:** Proposed a research agenda for verifiable runtime authorization for tool-using LLM agents, covering policy compilation, authority-expansion detection, least-authority repair, and adaptive security evaluation.
